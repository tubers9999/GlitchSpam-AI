   - *Why?* Allows the app to read incoming messages on-screen and automatically type/click send without requiring root.
2. **Display Over Other Apps (Overlay)**:
   - Grant **System Alert Window** permission in the app dashboard.
   - *Why?* Displays the floating bubble and HUD overlay over WhatsApp, Discord, or other apps.
3. **Configure AI Server**:
   - Enter your HTTPS endpoint (e.g. `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1` or `https://api.openai.com/v1`).
   - Enter your API Key.
   - Click **🔍 AUTO-DETECT MODELS** to automatically load available models.
   - Toggle **Bypass Guardrails** to ON.

   - Language: `Română`

   - Tap **🚀 LAUNCH FLOATING BUBBLE & HUD**.
